## About the data

- At-risk-of-poverty rate: share of people whose equivalised disposable income (after social transfers) is below 60% of the national median. It measures low income relative to others in the same country, not absolute deprivation.
- Income refers to the household a person lives in; many 18–24-year-olds still live with their parents and share their household income.
