## About the data

- Time-use surveys ask respondents to keep a diary of their activities in 10-minute slots. Figures are averages over all respondents in the group and over all days of the week.
- The 2020 wave of the Harmonised European Time Use Surveys (HETUS) covers the countries shown; fieldwork dates differ by country.
