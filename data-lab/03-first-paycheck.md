## About the data

- The Structure of Earnings Survey (SES) is run every four years; 2022 is the latest wave. It covers enterprises with at least 10 employees and excludes public administration (NACE section O).
- Mean gross hourly earnings include overtime pay. PPS is an artificial currency that equalises purchasing power across countries.
