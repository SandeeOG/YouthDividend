## About the data

- Population aged 15–24 is the sum of the male and female 15–19 and 20–24 age groups. Projections are the UN World Population Prospects medium variant, as published by the World Bank.
- Projections are not forecasts: they depend on assumptions about fertility, mortality and migration.
