## About the data

- Unemployment and participation are ILO modelled estimates, which fill gaps in national data to produce comparable series for all countries. Recent years are estimates and are revised as survey data arrive.
- Youth unemployment is measured against the labour force (those working or looking for work), not the whole youth population; students not seeking work are outside the labour force. NEET is measured against the whole population aged 15–24.
