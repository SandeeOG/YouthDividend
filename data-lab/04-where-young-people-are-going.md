## About the data

- Immigration and emigration count people changing their usual residence for at least 12 months, including nationals returning or leaving.
- Emigration is typically harder to record than immigration (people do not always deregister), so net figures may overstate inflows. Some countries record age in completed years and others as age reached during the year; each country is used under the definition it reports.
